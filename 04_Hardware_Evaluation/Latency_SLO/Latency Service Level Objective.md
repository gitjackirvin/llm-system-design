

| 4.1.a) Latency Service Level Objective (SLO) Explanation: The system is designed to maintain predictable response times across different usage scenarios, including classroom activity, CTF competition loads, business environments, and events. These latency targets include queueing, inference, and network overhead. To meet these goals the section below will define the latency SLO’s that will be met. Latency Service Level Objectives: \- Daily usage: Near-instant responses, with fast completion for typical requests  \- Normal usage: Responsive performance for both light and moderately heavy queries,  \- Peak usage: Maintained responsiveness even during high concurrent load by queuing responses. Response times are expected to vary depending on workload complexity, with lighter requests completing faster and heavier requests taking longer due to increased processing requirements. |
