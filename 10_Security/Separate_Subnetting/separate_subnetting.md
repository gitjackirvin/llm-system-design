10.6. Separate Subnetting
Explanation: This section explains the importance of separate subnetting and how it aligns with industry best-practices.

Separate subnetting allows all servers to operate on their own dedicated VLAN. This limits lateral movement, prevents a compromise from spreading, reduces broadcast domains, and lowers the overall attack surface. By keeping each segment independent, unauthorized access is contained to a single VLAN if one becomes compromised. 
Importance:  Network segmentation strengthens security by ensuring that a breach in one segment cannot easily impact others. This increases security posture and also limits lateral movement.
