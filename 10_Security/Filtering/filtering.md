Explanation: This section explains industry best‑practice filtering methods, how they should be applied, and where filtering will take place. 

Filtering is provided on a case‑by‑case basis. At the FastAPI gateway, both requests and responses are evaluated against custom content‑filtering rules that can be created as needed. These rules ensure that sensitive data, restricted content, and policy‑violating requests are blocked or modified so no data leakage occurs. 
Importance: Filtering is important because it prevents policy violations, data loss, and non‑permitted information from reaching an end user.
