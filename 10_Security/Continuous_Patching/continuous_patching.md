10.7. Continuous Patching
Explanation: This section explains the importance of continuous patching on all systems.

All operating systems, firmware, software, and components in the LLM system should be updated as soon as patches are available to address security vulnerabilities and reduce the overall attack surface. This includes applying fixes to any service or component in the environment to ensure issues are resolved before they can be exploited. 
Importance: Continuous patching eliminates risk provided by known vulnerabilities, strengthens system security, and helps maintain reliable operations.
